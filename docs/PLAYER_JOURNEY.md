# The Grimroot Trials: start to finish

Updated 27 November 2026, Australia/Sydney. This describes the implemented route and identifies the remaining differences from the intended full story.

## Arrival and the marks

A small invitation leads to member signup/login. All assigned marks on the current page then stay visible. The next required mark pulses yellow. Touch it once to wake it, then again to save it. It stays visible and dims. Out-of-order touches cannot advance progress. The member gem shows saved progress, the next page and earned display titles. All fifty marks use artwork and 44 by 44 CSS-pixel targets.

Each guardian offers optional sourced oddities, a conversation about the shop's backstage crew, and a reminder of the trail. Completed guardians can be revisited without collecting another mark or resetting anything. Grumblebark's age changes when challenged; his height remains a grievance. Tanglebright remains the final antagonist.

The supplied 253-fact CSV is retained with provenance. Its 190 source-checked whisper-eligible entries form four guardian pools; 63 learning-only entries stay outside the playful fact feed. The 289-row topic index supplies topic metadata, not additional invented facts. The older 761/805 count notes are historical references. All 17 named specialist roles appear across the four optional crew conversations. The autonomy notes supply themes of rest, saved footing, evidence and finishing one step at a time.

## The Omens Retreat

The thirty clues release on the established campaign calendar in Sydney time. A member starts with their first unfinished released clue, so late arrivals can catch up in order. A correct answer reveals an oddity with its source. If another clue is already released, Next released clue continues the trail. Wrong answers retain the existing attempt limit. These clues award no loyalty points and do not replace the fifty-mark sequence.

## The Last Ember

All fifty saved marks unlock the cave. Tanglebright receives the traveller at the fire. Optional sourced facts and crew conversations leave the saved reward dialogue alone. The player can ask who he is, hear the treasure offer, choose 500 Wix Loyalty points or the LOOTGREM checkout reward, change a choice before confirmation, or leave and return. Loading a page and reading a fact never claim a reward.

Reward issuance is currently disabled. Confirming records/checks the chosen route but does not issue points or reveal a checkout code. Rewards cannot honestly be described as live until the provider path is enabled and verified.

## Remaining story systems

The intended continuation is prestige, The Forgotten Chronicle after the first prestige, and 250 LP at the end of the final prestige. These are established story requirements, not completed runtime features in the current code.

Guardian thresholds currently unlock encounters; the mark sequence itself is not stopped at 20/30/40 until a guardian is completed. Cave entry currently checks fifty marks, rather than requiring all four encounters too. Milestone titles are automatic display achievements, not assigned Wix security roles/badges. Forced softgates, actual role/badge assignment, live reward issuance and the prestige/Chronicle finale remain to be implemented and tested separately.

The obsolete Home - portal goblin page is retired. The public /home-1 address has a server-side redirect to the canonical homepage.
